❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order? +
Select your product, add it to the cart, continue to checkout and provide your shipping information. Currently, Cash on Delivery is available.
What payment method is available? +
Sei Mart currently supports Cash on Delivery (COD).
How can I check my order? +
Enter your Order ID in the Track Your Order section above to find your order details.
Can I change my shipping address? +
You can update your saved shipping address from your Account page. For an order that has already been placed, contact support as soon as possible.
How can I request a return or refund? +
Contact Sei Mart support with your Order ID and explain the reason for your return or refund request.
I have another problem. What should I do? +
Submit a Support Request below or contact Sei Mart support.
